🎟️ Before you go · Tickets & practical tips

Admission: Approximately ₩26,000 per adult (around ₩52,000 for two) for the jjimjil spa, generally up to 6 hours. The water park is a separate ticket. Prices and terms may change.

Booking: Walk-ins are generally accepted; advance booking is optional. Sunday can be busy, so allow time for a queue. Prepaid tickets may offer a discount but do not necessarily grant priority entry.

Bring: Payment card, phone, hair tie if needed, skincare/moisturizer and personal hair products. Optional: fresh underwear, socks and your own washcloth or towel.

Provided / facilities: Spa clothing and lockers are generally included; towels and toiletries vary, and some towels may cost extra. Check the current onsite policy. Follow the posted shoe-locker instructions promptly after ticketing.

What to wear: No swimsuit is needed for the jjimjil spa. Wear the provided clothing in mixed-gender sauna and lounge areas. Bathing and changing areas are gender-separated; nude bathing is customary.

Etiquette: Shower before entering the baths, speak quietly in relaxation rooms, and never photograph changing or bathing areas.

Time limit: Additional time beyond the included six hours has been listed at ₩5,000 per extra hour; confirm on arrival. Your planned 7:30–9:45 PM visit is comfortably within the limit.
