AFTERNOON · STEP-BY-STEP SUBWAY

🚇 Mercure Hongdae → Namseoul Church

ONE TRANSFER · LINE 2 → LINE 9 LOCALAllow 45–60 min door-to-door · Depart 12:20–12:30 PM
1

Hotel → Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9

Leave Mercure at 144 Yanghwa-ro. Walk along Yanghwa-ro to the Hongik University Station Exit 9 (홍대입구역 9번 출구) entrance, about 2 minutes. Go down the stairs/escalator. Follow signs for Subway Line 2 · green circle — not AREX or the Gyeongui–Jungang Line.

2

Green Line 2 → Dangsan (당산역)

Tap your T-money card at the gates. Board Line 2 in the direction of Hapjeong (합정). Ride 2 stops: Hongik Univ. → Hapjeong → Dangsan.

3

At Dangsan: transfer to Line 9

Follow signs marked Line 9 · gold. Stay inside the station and follow the transfer passage; do not exit to the street. Choose the train toward Sinbanpo / Sinnonhyeon, and make sure it is a LOCAL (일반열차) train.

4

Gold Line 9 LOCAL → Sinbanpo (신반포역)

Stay on the all-stop train until Sinbanpo. Do not take the express (급행) — it skips Sinbanpo. Check the train's stopping pattern on the platform display.

5

Sinbanpo Exit 1 → church

Follow signs to Exit 1, then walk roughly 5–10 minutes to Namseoul Church (남서울교회), 36 Sinbanpo-ro 19-gil. Aim to arrive by 1:20 PM.

Subway cheat sheet: 2 Hongik Univ. → Dangsan · transfer · 9 LOCAL → Sinbanpo · Exit 1 · walk to church.

🚇 Lower-cost transit alternative

  1. Walk from church to Sinbanpo Station (신반포역).
  2. Take Line 9 LOCAL toward Dangsan (당산); express trains skip Sinbanpo.
  3. Transfer at Dangsan to Line 2 toward Hongik Univ. (홍대입구), 2 stops via Hapjeong.
  4. From Hongik Univ., take a taxi for the final uphill section (roughly 15–25 minutes depending on traffic). This mixed route is usually easier than navigating the hill by bus.

Allow roughly 60–90 minutes for the subway + taxi combination, including station access and waiting. Check live directions.

4:30–5:30
Travel to sauna

Taxi recommended; arrival depends on traffic.

5:30–6:00
Check in, change, shower

Separate men's/women's bath areas; shared heated rooms use provided sauna clothing.

6:00–7:30
Charcoal-kiln rooms + rest

Begin with gentler heat, hydrate, and take breaks. Very hot kiln rooms can burn skin.

7:30–8:15
Traditional sauna snacks

Try roasted sweet potatoes or rice cakes if offered, plus sikhye (sweet rice punch). Snacks cost extra.

8:15–9:00
Finish up and head back

Leave enough time before the earlier published 9 PM closing.

💳 What to budget and bring

Entry: ₩15,000/adult according to VisitKorea (verify on the day). Extras: snacks, food, treatments and some amenities may cost more. Bring: small towel/toiletries if preferred, fresh underwear, water, payment card and some cash. Remove jewelry before heat rooms; bring socks for foot protection if trying hotter rooms, but avoid the hottest kiln unless properly equipped.

Atmosphere: rustic and traditional, not a polished luxury spa. The charcoal kilns and roasting snacks are the highlight.
