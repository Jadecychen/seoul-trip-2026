🚕 Church → sauna: simplest route

TAXI RECOMMENDED · ~35–60 MINFrom Banpo to Seodaemun; traffic can be heavy on Saturday evening.
  1. Outside Namseoul Church, call a taxi using Kakao T or ask the driver for 숲속한방랜드 (Supsok Hanbang Land).
  2. Show the Korean address 서울 서대문구 봉원동 51.
  3. Check live fare/ETA in the taxi app; expect a variable metered fare, not a fixed quoted price.

🚇 Lower-cost transit alternative

  1. Walk from church to Sinbanpo Station (신반포역).
  2. Take Line 9 LOCAL toward Dangsan (당산); express trains skip Sinbanpo.
  3. Transfer at Dangsan to Line 2 toward Hongik Univ. (홍대입구), 2 stops via Hapjeong.
  4. From Hongik Univ., take a taxi for the final uphill section (roughly 15–25 minutes depending on traffic). This mixed route is usually easier than navigating the hill by bus.

Allow roughly 60–90 minutes for the subway + taxi combination, including station access and waiting. Check live directions.

💳 What to budget and bring

Entry: ₩15,000/adult according to VisitKorea (verify on the day). Extras: snacks, food, treatments and some amenities may cost more. Bring: small towel/toiletries if preferred, fresh underwear, water, payment card and some cash. Remove jewelry before heat rooms; bring socks for foot protection if trying hotter rooms, but avoid the hottest kiln unless properly equipped.

Atmosphere: rustic and traditional, not a polished luxury spa. The charcoal kilns and roasting snacks are the highlight.

🎟️ Before you go · Tickets & practical tips

Admission: Approximately ₩26,000 per adult (around ₩52,000 for two) for the jjimjil spa, generally up to 6 hours. The water park is a separate ticket. Prices and terms may change.

Booking: Walk-ins are generally accepted; advance booking is optional. Sunday can be busy, so allow time for a queue. Prepaid tickets may offer a discount but do not necessarily grant priority entry.

Bring: Payment card, phone, hair tie if needed, skincare/moisturizer and personal hair products. Optional: fresh underwear, socks and your own washcloth or towel.

Provided / facilities: Spa clothing and lockers are generally included; towels and toiletries vary, and some towels may cost extra. Check the current onsite policy. Follow the posted shoe-locker instructions promptly after ticketing.

What to wear: No swimsuit is needed for the jjimjil spa. Wear the provided clothing in mixed-gender sauna and lounge areas. Bathing and changing areas are gender-separated; nude bathing is customary.

Etiquette: Shower before entering the baths, speak quietly in relaxation rooms, and never photograph changing or bathing areas.

Time limit: Additional time beyond the included six hours has been listed at ₩5,000 per extra hour; confirm on arrival. Your planned 4:45–7:30 PM visit is comfortably within the limit.
